Typography

One typeface.
Three weights.

Thorwallet uses a single corporate typeface — OT Sono by Oh no Type Co. — across everything: display, body, UI, data. Three weights cover the whole system.

04.1 — The typeface

OT Sono.

A geometric sans with even stroke width and a quietly mechanical feel. Sono carries both the brand voice and the working interface — without ever needing a second family.

Semibold · 600

Regular · 400

04.4 — Voice in use

Weight does the work.

Without a second face, hierarchy comes from weight, size, and tracking. Lead with Semibold; let Regular carry the rest.
