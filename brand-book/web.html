The website,
spec'd.

The living system behind thorwallet.org — the outline-accent headline, the scroll-driven device hero, the frosted mega-menu, editorial plan cards, rate tables, the FAQ pattern, dual light/dark themes, and the social & SEO layer. This is the source of truth for every marketing build.

08.1 — Signature headline

The outline-accent keyword.

Every section headline is white ink with one keyword carrying the brand. On dark surfaces the keyword is a cyan-stroked outline; on mobile (and any small/busy context) it switches to a solid signal-gradient fill for legibility. One accented keyword per headline — never two.

Desktop · outline

The best crypto wallet for Swaps & DeFi.

Accepted in 175+ countries.

Mobile · gradient fill

The best crypto wallet for Swaps & DeFi.

08.2 — Hero & the device

A phone that moves with the scroll.

The homepage hero centres the gradient lockup, the outline headline and the download CTAs over a scroll-driven device animation. As the user scrolls, the phone scrubs through a frame sequence while short lines fly in — "Swap across every chain", then "Your money. Always."

App screen
DesktopLandscape frame sequence, pinned, scrubbed to scroll progress
Mobile / iPadPortrait sequence; phone peeks from the bottom, scrubs upward
TextLede + 2 fly-in panels, opacity/translate keyed to scroll
Reduced motionJumps to final frame — no scrubbing
Device chromeSilent — no notch, glare, tilt or perspective skew

The device is the imagery. Keep it centred and upright; let the screen do the work. Never composite it onto stock photography or rotate it.

08.6 — FAQ pattern

Questions, answered.

FAQs use native <details> accordions — a display-weight question with a signal-green +/× toggle, hairline dividers, and an answer that eases in. Always paired with FAQPage structured data so the Q&As are eligible for rich results and cited accurately by AI answer engines.

08.7 — Light & dark themes

One brand, two modes.

The site ships dark by default with an opt-in light theme, toggled from the nav and remembered across visits (an inline script applies it before first paint, so there's no flash). Light is Apple-clean: white canvas, navy ink, a deeper accent and signal-text gradient that stay readable, and monochrome logos flipped to black.

08.8 — Buttons & CTAs

Three actions, no more.

Primary actions use the royal-blue fill (matching the app). The signal gradient is reserved for the occasional hero/"Get started" accent — one per view. Ghost buttons handle secondary actions. All download CTAs — nav, hero, footer — point to the referral link.
