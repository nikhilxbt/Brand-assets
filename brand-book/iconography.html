Iconography

A line
system.

A monoweight stroked icon family designed to sit alongside Sono. Geometric, calm, never decorative — covering wallet, swap, earn, multisig and card surfaces.

06.1 — Construction

One stroke. One radius.

All icons live on a 24-unit grid with a 1.5-unit stroke and 2u corner radius. Terminals are rounded.

Grid
24 × 24 units · 2u safe-area
Stroke
1.5u monoweight
Corners
2u radius · rounded terminals
Style
Geometric · two-state (line / fill on active)
Color

06.3 — Sizing

Four ratified sizes.

Don't draw between these. Each size has a designated stroke weight to maintain optical balance.

06.4 — States

Default & Active.

Two visual states. Active swaps the stroke for a fill in the primary blue. Avoid building a third state for hover — use color change instead.
