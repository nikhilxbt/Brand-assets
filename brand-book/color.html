Color

Navy, blue
& a signal.

A deep navy surface system carries the product. Royal blue is the single primary — every key action. Mint green marks positive value and highlights. The cyan→green signal lives in the logo and small accents. The marketing site ships dark by default with an opt-in light theme (see 03.2).

03.2 — Light theme

One brand, two modes.

The website is dark by default with an opt-in light theme, toggled from the nav and remembered across visits (applied before first paint, so there is no flash). Light is Apple-clean: white canvas, navy ink, a deepened accent and a darker signal-text gradient that stay readable, and monochrome logos flipped to black. Dark remains the primary brand surface.

03.3 — Primary & Accent

Blue acts. Green confirms.

Royal blue is the one primary — buttons, the Trade FAB, active tabs, links. Mint green is reserved for positive values, success, and the occasional highlight. Never use green as a button fill.

03.7 — Mix Ratios

The 60/30/10 rule.

Keep designs grounded by following these proportions. Saturated accents are the seasoning, never the dish.

60%
Surface

Abyss, Slate or Harbor navy. The dominant tone of every screen.

30%
Ink

White type, hairlines and structural shapes. The information layer.

10%
Action

Royal blue for actions, mint green for positive — used as emphasis only.
