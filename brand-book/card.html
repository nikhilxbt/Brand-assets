11.5 - Specs

Getting it right.

Ratio
1.586 : 1ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1. Never stretch or crop the card face.
Corner radius
About 3.5% of card widthMatch the physical card. Do not use the UI radius tokens.
Marks
Chevron centre, Mastercard top right, wordmark bottom rightThe positions are fixed. Do not move, recolour or add marks.
Shadow
Soft and deep, straight down0 20px 40px at 50% black on navy. No coloured glow behind the card.
Angle
Flat, or two cards at -8° / +6°The hero pairing above is the only tilted layout we use.
11.6 - Do & don't

Protect the card.

Do
  • Lead with Sunrise when showing a single card.
  • Show a realistic but fake balance and card number (***5397).
  • Keep the card the brightest thing on the canvas.
Don't
  • Invent new colourways or gradients.
  • Put the signal gradient on top of the card.
  • Show real card numbers, names or expiry dates.
  • Mix metal renders and digital cards in one layout.
