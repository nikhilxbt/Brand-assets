01.2 — Principles

How we think.

Decisions across product, design and copy run through these four filters.

P · 01

Plain over polished.

Crypto is full of jargon and theatre. We strip both away. If a sentence needs a glossary, rewrite it.

P · 02

Mountains, not moonshots.

Our metaphors come from the Swiss landscape — solid, lasting, earned. Not rockets, lambos, or hype.

P · 03

The user owns the keys.

Every interaction reaffirms control. Defaults are conservative; risky actions take two confirmations.

P · 04

Clarity is a feature.

Numbers are never decorative. Currency, fees, and balances are always sized for reading first.

01.3 — Voice

How we sound.

Direct. Confident. Technical but plain-spoken. We don't sell crypto — we sell autonomy. Use these examples as calibration when writing UI strings, marketing copy, or documentation.

Do — straight

"Your money. Your keys. Your call."

Short. Owns the relationship. No jargon.

Don't — hype

"Unleash your wealth on the next-gen, AI-powered Web3 super-app! 🚀"

Empty intensifiers, emoji theatre, no actual claim.

Do — concrete

"Swap BTC for SOL in one move. No bridges. No wrapped tokens."

Names the verbs. Says what's true.

Don't — vague

"Empowering everyone, everywhere, in the new financial paradigm."

"Empowering" is a tell. So is "paradigm".

Do — calm under risk

"This transaction is irreversible. Confirm to send 0.5 ETH."

States the consequence, then asks. No fear, no fluff.

Don't — alarm

"⚠️ DANGER! You may lose all funds! Are you SURE?!"

Capital letters and emoji are not warnings — they're noise.

01.4 — Vocabulary

The words we use.

A small lexicon. Thorwallet is always one word, TitleCase T and W. The token is TITN, all caps.

Prefer

  • your wallet
  • self-custody
  • cross-chain · native swaps
  • send · receive · swap · earn
  • network fee
  • all chains, all yield, one app

Avoid

  • HODL
  • degen
  • moon, ape, lambo
  • "revolution", "disrupt"
  • gas (use "network fee")
  • web3 super-app
