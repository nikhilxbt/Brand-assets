09.5 — Files & Hand-off

Master files.

Always supply both vector and raster. Pantone for spot, CMYK for offset, RGB for screen.

Vector masters

.svg · .pdf · .ai

For all logo, type, and gradient assets. Outline strokes before hand-off; preserve named layers.

Raster exports

.png · .jpg · .webp

PNG @ 1×, 2×, 3× for digital. JPG quality 92 for photography. WebP for web only.

09.6 — Pre-flight

Final-check list.

Every print job goes through this list before sign-off.

Logo uses an approved lockup (full-color, ink, or white).
Clear-space respects the mark-height rule.
Type is OT Sono — no substitutes (except Arial in email).
Color values are Pantone / CMYK, not screen RGB.
Imagery follows the six-rule approach in section 05.
Bleed and safe-area honored per format.
Final PDF is X-1a:2001 with embedded fonts.
Stock and finish are specified on the job ticket.
∞

That's the system.

Nine sections, one wallet. For revisions, exceptions, or new applications — write to brand@thorwallet.org.
