02.1 — Construction

Mark, then wordmark.

The mark is a fixed vector asset — two chamfered blades meeting at the centre, filled with the brand gradient. It locks to the wordmark with the mark's own width as the gap. Never redraw the blades or retype the wordmark; always use the supplied artwork.

Gradient mark · vector asset
Lock to
Wordmark · tight tracking
02.2 — Clearspace

Always give it room.

Minimum clearspace around the full logo equals the height of the mark. Around the standalone mark, equals one-third of its width. Nothing else lives inside this zone.

02.3 — Color Lockups

Approved color treatments.

Three lockups cover 95% of cases. Default to the full-color logo on a dark surface. Use the mono ink version on light or print surfaces, and the mono white version on the sunset gradient or busy photography.

Full color on dark — primary
Mono ink on bone — light / print
Mono white on gradient — campaign

On the light-theme website, keep the gradient mark but switch the wordmark to navy ink (the darktext lockup) — and the nav mark flips to solid black. Never place the white wordmark on a light surface; never place the ink wordmark on dark.

02.5 — Incorrect Usage

Do not.

The mark is not a sandbox. Six things that break it:

Don't skew or italicize.

The logo has a fixed posture. No oblique versions exist.

Don't stretch or condense.

The proportions are fixed. Resize uniformly only.

Don't place on a single accent.

Use a dark surface, bone, or the full gradient — not one saturated hue.

Don't put the ink logo on dark.

Match the variant to the surface. Dark needs the full-color or white logo.

Don't add taglines to the lockup.

The logo stands alone. Set claims as separate type elements.

Don't apply shadows or effects.

Flat reproduction only. The mark is not a button.
