About this manual

This manual defines how Thorwallet looks, sounds, and behaves across every surface — from the app and the card to outdoor mockups and printed collateral. Use it as a working reference, not a museum piece.

The brand is built on three promises — Self-Custody, Cross-Chain Swaps, DeFi & Yield — anchored in Swiss craft and DeFi-native engineering. Every choice in this document points back to one of them.
