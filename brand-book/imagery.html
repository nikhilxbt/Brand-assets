05 · ImageryNew Brand Guidelines · v0.2
Imagery

The product
is the picture.

Thorwallet shows itself — app screens, gradient card art, token marks, and a playful reward world. There is no stock photography, no landscapes, no lifestyle scenes. The interface and its assets carry the brand.

05.1 — Approach

Six rules.

Every Thorwallet visual answers to all six.

I · 01

The product is the hero.

App screens in clean device frames are the primary imagery — not lifestyle photos.

I · 02

Dark by default.

Deep navy backgrounds with soft blue and green ambient glow. Never a light canvas.

I · 03

Color lives on the card.

Saturated color is reserved for the Mastercard art — one colorway per tier.

I · 04

Tokens are full-color.

Coin and asset marks keep their native colors, with a small chain badge.

I · 05

Rewards play.

The reward world is bright blue, sparkled, with illustrated 3D assets — a deliberate change of gear.

I · 06

No stock, no fakery.

No landscapes, no stock smiles, no CG renders pretending to be photographs.

05.5 — The reward world

A playful change of gear.

Rewards, Quests, the Wheel, Shop and Tap & Play live in a brighter royal-blue world with sparkles, gem motifs, and illustrated 3D assets. Currencies are TITN, SPARKS and tickets. This treatment is for gamification only — never the wallet or card surfaces.

05.6 — Ambient backgrounds

Glow, not grain.

Backgrounds are deep navy lit by soft radial glows — blue and green for the app, brighter blue for rewards. No noise, no texture, no photography.

05.7 — Do & Don't

Calibrate the eye.

What belongs, and what doesn't.

Do — the product in a clean frame

Real screens, navy glow, device chrome silent. The interface is the imagery.

Alpine photo
Don't — landscapes & stock

Mountains, lifestyle, and stock photography belong to other brands — not Thorwallet.

Do — illustrated reward assets

Bright blue, sparkles, gem-and-gold 3D art — but only in the reward world.

Don't — game art outside rewards

Keep chests, potions and mascots out of the wallet, swap, and card surfaces.
