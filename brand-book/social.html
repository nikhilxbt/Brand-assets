10.1 - Anatomy

Five parts, always in this order.

Taken from our latest post. Copy this structure for every launch, feature or update post.

1 2 3 4 5 6
1

Status eyebrow

Signal dot plus a short label in wide, spaced capitals: Coming soon, Android beta, Native USDC.

2

Two-line headline

SemiBold, tight tracking. The final word carries the cyan to green signal gradient.

3

One-sentence lead

Plain words, with the key action or name in bold. No more than two lines.

4

Three proof rows

Line icon in signal green, one fact each. Honest, including trade-offs ("Costs a little more").

5

Lockup footer

White THORWallet lockup, hairline divider, then partner or URL in muted capitals.

6

Real device

An actual app screenshot in a navy phone frame with a soft cyan to green edge glow. Never a mock UI.

10.2 - Rules

Six rules.

Every post we publish follows all six.

S · 01

Navy canvas.

#0A1A2B with a faint grid and blue and green ambient glow in the corners. Never a light background.

S · 02

Copy left, product right.

The text column sits on the left, around 55% of the width. The device or card takes the right side.

S · 03

Signal on one word.

Only the payoff word gets the gradient: "swaps.", "Free.", "100% free.". Never the whole line.

S · 04

Facts over hype.

Concrete numbers and plain claims. If there is a cost or a limit, say it.

S · 05

One call to action.

At most one pill (Early access, $0.00 swap fees) plus the URL. Nothing else competes.

S · 06

Export at 2x.

Design at 1600 x 900 and export at 3200 x 1800 so it stays sharp on X and in Telegram.

10.4 - Formats

Three sizes.

Most posts are 16:9. Release notes also get a square version, and the profile header is a wide banner.

16 : 9

Post, landscape

1600 x 900, export 3200 x 1800. Default for X, Telegram and LinkedIn.

Post, square

1080 x 1080. Release notes and carousels.

X header

1500 x 500. Keep copy inside the centre 60% because the avatar covers the bottom left.

10.5 - Do & don't

Keep it clean.

Do
  • Use real screenshots from the current build.
  • Put the gradient on one payoff word.
  • Keep proof rows to three.
  • Use a hyphen or a comma, never a long dash.
Don't
  • Stack several calls to action or competing pills.
  • Use stock photos, 3D coins or meme art.
  • Tilt, skew or add glare to the phone.
  • Set body copy in the gradient.
