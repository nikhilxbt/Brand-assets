FAQ

Swap questions, answered.

Are the swaps really native?

Yes. You send real BTC and receive real SOL on the destination chain. No bridges, no wrapped tokens - which means no bridge risk.

What do swaps cost?

Swaps under $100 are free. Above that, 0.2–1.25% depending on your $TITN staking tier - and stakers earn up to ~70% of fees back in USDC. Larger transactions unlock automatic volume discounts.

Do I need gas tokens on every chain?

Not on supported routes - swaps there are gasless, so you don't need to hold the destination chain's native token before you receive funds on it.

Which tokens can I swap?

20,000+ tokens across every major chain - Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain, Stellar, Sui, Cosmos, THORChain and more - with best-rate routing across aggregated liquidity.
