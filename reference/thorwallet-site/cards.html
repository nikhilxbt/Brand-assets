    FAQ

    Card questions, answered.

    Is the THORWallet card available in my country?

    The card works anywhere Mastercard is accepted, across 175+ countries including the United States. Most countries open instantly; a few require extra verification and a small number aren't served yet. Use the searchable list above to check your country.

    Do I keep self-custody of my crypto?

    Yes. Your wallet balance stays in self-custody - your keys never leave your device. You top up the card from your wallet when you want to spend, so you stay in control of your funds.

    How fast can I get a card?

    A virtual Basic card is ready in about five minutes, straight from the app. Swiss Banking adds a full Swiss IBAN and SEPA account.

    What are the fees?

    Crypto top-ups are 0% on Basic, and 0.25-1% on Swiss Banking. FX markup is 1.5% on Basic and 1% on Swiss Banking. ATM withdrawals cost $1 (domestic) or $5 (international) on Basic, and are not available on Swiss Banking.

    All fees are indicative and may vary by jurisdiction and verification status.

    Is there cashback?

    Premium earns up to 2% cashback on purchases, with higher rates for $TITN stakers. Basic and Swiss Banking don't include cashback.

    Which cryptocurrencies can I top up with?

    You can fund the card from your THORWallet balance across all supported chains and 20,000+ tokens. If you hold an asset that isn't the card's settlement currency, swap it in-app first, then top up.

    What is the Swiss Banking account?

    Swiss Banking gives you a personal Swiss IBAN and SEPA account with a multicurrency Mastercard in CHF, EUR, USD and RMB. You can send and receive like a traditional bank, with monthly limits up to $10M.

    Is it a real Mastercard?

    Yes. It's a Mastercard issued under licence and accepted anywhere Mastercard is, online and in store. Mastercard is a registered trademark of Mastercard International.
